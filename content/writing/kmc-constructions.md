KMC Constructions has been building for more than fifty years and has just started a new railway build. Ananya Reddy put every head of department in one room for a full day: projects, quantity surveying, supply chain, plant and equipment, accounts, internal audit, company secretarial, HR, tenders, IT.

The lesson: a hierarchy is already a trust system, and AI changes only one half of trust. It brings competence at scale, but no goodwill — so AI structures, people verify, and a named person always has the final word. Every head left with a spec for their own page of a shared project dashboard.
